# Pixel FX Pack v1.4.1 - Effect list

**Listing line:** 116 effects total = 54 from v1.3 (48 effects + 6 particle presets) + 20 "hell survivor" effects in v1.4 + 17 projectile-weapon effects in v1.5 + 3 chain/poison effects in v1.5.1 + 6 frost/reaction effects in v1.6.0 + 11 Ox Pit telegraph/hazard/status effects in v1.7.0 + 1 boss bullet in v1.8.5 + 1 pattern warning in v1.8.6 + 1 boss stun bar in v1.8.8 + 1 hook chain in v1.8.9 + 1 judge kit in v1.9.0, plus a horde performance toolkit (5 tools, not counted as effects).

How this is counted: one line = one thing you can switch on or call that looks different from every other line. Variants of one effect
(e.g. 4-way vs 8-way outline, outline width) are NOT counted separately. Particle presets are listed apart because they are tuned settings of one particle system.
Status: C# compile-checked against Unity 6 assemblies. **Shaders not yet compiled or seen in the Unity Editor** (see README).
The gallery mode in the demo (press G) shows these in this order with the same numbers.

## Sprite shader - `PixelFX/Sprite Uber` via the `PixelFX` component (stackable, all off by default)
1. **Outline** - 1-4 px outer outline, 4- or 8-way.
2. **Inner Outline** - outline drawn just inside the sprite edge.
3. **Hit Flash** - solid colour flash; `HitFlash` adds timed blinks.
4. **Pixel Dissolve** - per-texel dissolve with a glowing (HDR) edge; `DissolveFX.PlayOut/PlayIn`.
5. **Palette Swap** - LUT recolour, up to 16 colours per row; `PaletteSwap` builds the LUT for you.
6. **Silhouette** - flat colour fill; with `SeeThrough` + SpriteMask shows characters behind walls.
7. **Wave** - per-row wobble in whole-texel steps.
8. **Shine Sweep** - diagonal shine band; `ShineSweep` loops it.
9. **Pixelate** - snap the sprite to N-texel blocks.
10. **Drop Shadow** - hard offset shadow (needs transparent padding).
11. **Rim Light** - 1 px directional rim, as if lit by a torch/moon.
12. **Emission / Glow** - additive HDR colour; blooms with URP HDR + Bloom.
13. **Wind Sway** - vertex sway: top moves, feet pinned (grass, trees, cloaks).  *(v1.3)*
14. **Glitch** - random horizontal row tears, re-rolled ~12x/s.  *(v1.3)*
15. **Motion Blur** - 5-tap directional smear in whole texels.  *(v1.3)*
16. **Chromatic Aberration (sprite)** - red/blue split on one sprite.  *(v1.3)*
17. **Hologram** - tinted scanlines with flicker and transparency.  *(v1.3)*
18. **Frozen** - ice colour ramp with twinkling glints.  *(v1.3)*
19. **Burn** - bottom-up burn with charred band and glowing front.  *(v1.3)*
20. **Tint Pulse** - pulsing colour (poison, buff, low HP).  *(v1.3)*
21. **Gradient Map** - luminance mapped to a 3-colour ramp.  *(v1.3)*
22. **Greyscale** - desaturate (petrify, disabled, flashback).  *(v1.3)*
23. **Sepia** - old-photo tone.  *(v1.3)*
24. **Negative** - colour invert.  *(v1.3)*
25. **Rainbow** - scrolling hue cycle (star power-up).  *(v1.3)*
26. **Hue Shift** - rotate hue (enemy variants without a LUT).  *(v1.3)*
27. **Dither Fade** - 4x4 Bayer-pattern fade in/out, no alpha blending.  *(v1.3)*

## Horde performance toolkit (v1.4, not counted as effects) - Runtime/HordeFX.cs, PixelParticles
- **Batched hit flash** - `HordeFX.Hit/Flash(sr)` swaps to one shared flash material (no per-enemy MaterialPropertyBlock).
- **Batched dissolve** - `HordeFX.Kill(sr, onDone)` steps through 8 shared dissolve materials, then returns the sprite to you for pooling.
- **Pooled damage numbers** - `HordeFX.Number(pos, value, crit)` on one glyph atlas (0-9, x, !), cap 48 on screen.
- **Cheap death burst** - `HordeFX.CheapBurst(pos, color)` 5 pooled pixels, no particle system.
- **Particle budget** - `PixelParticles.autoBudget`: above 60% of max live particles, new emits are scaled down instead of dropped/stalling.
- **Stress scene** - `Demo/HordeStressTest.unity`: 400 enemies by default (+/-100 buttons, up to 2000), FPS + 5 s minimum, toggles.

## v1.5 projectile weapons (75-91) - `ProjectileFX` static API (Runtime/ProjectileFX.cs), pooled by `ProjectileFXRunner`
Survivor-style auto weapons (not guns). Every projectile = code-made 16x16 4-frame sprite + cast flash + element trail + impact, moved by one runner (1024 preallocated slots, no per-spawn allocation, 2 shared materials). Hits use `Physics2D.OverlapCircle` with a preallocated buffer; hook damage with `ProjectileFX.OnHit`, homing/bounce targeting with `ProjectileFX.FindTarget`. `ProjShot` struct + `ProjectileFX.Preset(element)` for custom weapons.
75. **Fireball** - `Fireball(pos, dir)` flickering teardrop, rising ember trail, explodes into a mini nova + hit-stop.
76. **Blood Bolt** - `BloodBolt(pos, dir)` glossy blood drop, dripping trail, blood splash with falling droplets.
77. **Bone Shard** - `BoneShard(pos, dir)` spinning thrown bone, bone-chip impact (great with `Spread`).
78. **Soul Orb** - `SoulOrb(pos, dir)` homing blue soul with orbiting sparkle.
79. **Curse Skull** - `CurseSkull(pos, dir)` slow purple flaming skull, infinite pierce, cursed explosion.
80. **Hellfire Spear** - `HellfireSpear(pos, dir)` fast long spear, pierces 3.
81. **Lightning Bolt** - `LightningBolt(pos, dir)` re-rolled jagged bolt, bounces 4 times between enemies.
82. **Homing Magic Missile** - `MagicMissile(pos, dir)` sharp-turning arcane missile.
83. **Returning Blade** - `ReturningBlade(pos, dir, owner)` spinning crescent, slows, returns to the player (boomerang).
84. **Piercing Beam** - `Beam(from, dir, lengthPx, element)` instant 2-layer beam, sparks along the line, hits everything on it.
85. **Bouncing Projectile** - `Bouncing(element, bounces, pos, dir)` any element, retargets nearest enemy.
86. **Splitting Projectile** - `Splitting(element, children, pos, dir)` splits into a ring of children on first hit.
87. **Cast / Spawn Flash** - `CastFlash(pos, dir, element)` 2-frame white pop + directional sparks + recoil kick; ring cast for volleys.
88. **Element Trails** - per-element 2px->1px stepped trail (fire rises, blood drips, lightning short-lived).
89. **Impact / Explosion** - `Impact(pos, element, vel)` pop, ring, element debris, shake/kick; fire/hellfire/curse explode bigger.
90. **Pierce / Bounce / Split Hits** - `HitPierce`, `HitBounce`, `HitSplit` distinct hit reads so players see what their build does.
91. **Pattern Helpers** - `Spread`, `Ring`, `SpiralStep`, `HomingVolley` (bullet-hell patterns for player or boss).

## v1.5.1 chains & poison (92-94) - `ChainFX` static API (Runtime/ChainFX.cs), pooled by `ChainFXRunner`
Sized for 240x135 art with a ~15px hero (1px bolts, 4x3 links, 6-24px puddles). 48 instance slots x 32 preallocated sprites, one shared atlas texture, additive + default sprite materials only.
92. **Chain Lightning Jump** - `ChainFX.ChainLightning(origin, jumps, jumpRangePx, firstRangePx)`: jagged 1px bolt jumps to the nearest Collider2D then nearest un-hit one (max 6 hops), 3-frame hit spark, shake + tiny hit-stop, `ChainFX.OnChainHit(collider, hop)`. Also `ChainLightningPoints` for scripted paths. Upgrade of #62: `HellFX.ChainLightning(points, ...)` keeps its signature but now forwards to this pooled bolt (no allocations, gains hit sparks).
93. **Chain Whip Sweep** - `ChainFX.ChainWhip(origin, fromDeg, toDeg, lengthPx, seconds, follow)`: iron-link chain swings an arc with weighted lag, 2-step tip trail, tip hits colliders once each (`ChainFX.OnWhipHit`) with spark.
94. **Poison Puddle** - `ChainFX.PoisonPuddle(pos, radiusPx, seconds, tickSeconds)` returns a handle; grows in 3 steps, loops 6 bubbles (dot, 2x2, pop ring), shrinks/fades; `seconds <= 0` loops until `ChainFX.StopPuddle(handle)`; `ChainFX.OnPuddleTick(collider)` damage ticks inside the ellipse.

## v1.6.0 frost & reactions (95-100) - `ReactionFX` static API (Runtime/ReactionFX.cs), pooled by `ReactionFXRunner`
Same pattern as ChainFX: 48 instance slots x 48 preallocated sprites, one shared 64x32 atlas, additive + default sprite materials only, no per-spawn allocations. Status effects never touch the target's material, so HordeFX batching stays intact. Statuses share `ReactionFXRunner.StatusCap` (default 32).
95. **Frost Cleave** - `ReactionFX.FrostCleave(origin, dirDeg, radiusPx = 18, arcDeg = 150, seconds = 0.22f, clockwise = true, follow = null, color = null, order = 70)`: icy, tintable crescent sweep (thin-to-thick band, white leading edge, inner frost line, ice motes), hits Collider2Ds inside the swept wedge once each (`ReactionFX.OnCleaveHit`, max 16 per swing). Pooled replacement for the white-only `PixelFXSpawn.Slash` for this use.
96. **Shock Status** - `ReactionFX.ShockStatus(target, seconds = 2f, radiusPx = 7, centerYPx = 0, color = null, order = 60)` returns a handle: two looping 3-segment zaps around the target with end sparks; `seconds <= 0` loops until `ReactionFX.StopStatus(handle)`; ends itself if the target is destroyed/inactive.
97. **Overload** - `ReactionFX.Overload(pos, radiusPx = 28, seconds = 0.5f, juice = true, fire = null, shock = null, order = 66)`: Burn+Shock reaction, white core -> gold -> fire -> ember ring, inner fire ring, 5 electric spokes, sparks; shake 2.5px + hit-stop 0.06s scaled by `ProjectileFX.ShakeScale/HitStopScale`; `ReactionFX.OnOverloadHit(collider)` called synchronously for up to 32 colliders in radius; returns the hit count.
98. **Bleed Status** - `ReactionFX.BleedStatus(target, seconds = 3f, bodyHeightPx = 8, feetYPx = -7, color = null, order = 60)` returns a handle: 3 looping drips fall and splat at the feet; same handle/Stop/cap rules as Shock.
99. **Frost Shatter** - `ReactionFX.FrostShatter(pos, shards = 10, radiusPx = 14, color = null, order = 64)`: 3-14 spinning ice shards with gravity + flash + frost ring, 0.6s. Use on death of a frozen/chilled enemy.
100. **Elite Chest Open** - `ReactionFX.EliteChestOpen(pos, seconds = 1.6f, columnPx = 48, coins = 6, bloodGlints = 3, gold = null, order = 75)`: flash, gold light column (3 layers, flicker, narrows), lid pops off and spins, rising sparkles, up to 8 coins and 4 blood glints bounce out and glint. Does not hide or swap your chest sprite.
Juice hooks: `ProjectileFX.ShakeScale`, `HitStopScale`, `FlashScale`. Setup: `HellFX.PPU = 16; ReactionFXRunner.I.Prewarm();` (2304 sprites).
