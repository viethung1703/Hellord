# t1481 fire basics v2 — source licences

All layers used in this pack are CC0 1.0 or U.S. government public-domain recordings,
plus original procedural synthesis. Licences were checked on each file's own page
(OpenGameArt, NPS Yellowstone Sound Library, Internet Archive / USFWS).
No Freesound account was used. No game audio was ripped or reused.

Raw recordings are reused from `../_sources/` (the t1481 v1 source tree).
A few of those files are themselves copies also stored under `../../d2feel_t1449/_sources/`.
This v2 folder does not duplicate the raw packs. v1 files were not overwritten.

Feel research (PoE 2 Molten Blast, D2/D2R Molten Boulder, D2R fire skills) is
documented in `RESEARCH.md`. No reference footage was kept or sampled.

## Procedural layers
Original synthesis (numpy / scipy): sub thump, wet thud, whoosh, rumble, mid grind, fire roar, fire body, crackle bursts, crunch / grit, soft saturation. These are not recorded from any game.

## Licence notes / doubts
- NPS and USFWS pages say 'credit if possible / where appropriate'. That is a courtesy, not a licence condition; attribution is listed above anyway.
- rubberduck pack #2 is titled '80 CC0 creture SFX #2' on OpenGameArt (author typo).
- Maple Fire 'afternoon' and Beehive Geyser clips in `../_sources/` were not used (grasshoppers / too quiet), same as v1.
- No new third-party packs were added in v2. Freesound-derived and CC-BY / mixed packs skipped in v1 remain unused.
