3. RULES FOR CODE (spawn / sort / move)
=====================================================================================================
MOLTEN SPIT
  1. t0 cast: caster plays gouge (16 f, flip X for west). Rock is spawned at the hand (pivot + 9 px toward the target, x; z lift 11 px) at t0+16 f.
  2. Move FLAT in a straight line at 160 px/s (10 u/s), smoothstep NOT used in game (linear); z constant 11 px (screen y = ground y - 11). Row = DirRow(v) with the screen-space velocity. Shadow decal on the ground at the rock's ground x,y.
  3. SORT: rock sortKey = ground y + 30 (it must NEVER hide behind imps; v2 sorted by y only). Impact sortKey = y + 40. Strands y + 20, stars y + 40. Heat/ember patches are DECALS under all actors.
  4. Arrival (or first enemy within 22 px / 1.4 u): play impact (10 f; fireball visible f0-5), apply damage in the 22 px radius.
  5. impact+3: spawn 8 strands (+1 f stagger on odd), directions = throw dir +-35 deg in 10 deg steps, reach 74-116 px, movement ease-out (1-(1-t)^1.7) over 10 f, then play the fade frames 6-13 at the tip. A star plays at each tip from strand frame 9. Strands are cosmetic (no damage) unless design wants a small shard hit: use a 6 px radius.
  6. impact+1: heat sprite decal for 30 f. impact+4: ember_patch_v1 (75 f life). At strand landing: every second strand spawns ember_patch_v2/v3 (75 f). Patch strips: play ignite 0-5 once, LOOP 6-17 until life-18 f, then die frames.
  7. Spam: each cast is independent; cap ember patches at ~24 alive (oldest dies early) so the floor never washes out.
INFERNAL BOULDER
  1. t0 cast: cast ring on the caster (24 f). At t0+24 the ball is spawned 12 px forward of the caster (pivot y + 5 toward the aim); 4 band tiles are laid behind it at once (offsets -24..-6 px), so the band starts ~1.3 D long.
  2. Move at 53 px/s (3.3 u/s) in a straight line for 60 f max (106 px / 6.6 u). Do NOT use lerp from start to end in 0.4 s like v2 did. Rotation frame from distance (see above). Row = DirRow(move dir).
  3. Every 6 px of travel lay a band tile at the ball's ground point. Tile i plays ignite 6 f, loop, then die; life 60 f (2.0 s); oldest dies first because they were laid first. Tiles are DECAL-layer (sortKey = -999), the ball sortKey = ground y + 14 (always above flames and above imps' feet), the end blast = y + 40.
  4. Any enemy overlapped by the ball is pushed along the roll direction (+ a small recoil table 0,3,4,3,2,1,1,0 px over 8 f); no reference footage for hit/knock-back exists, so this is a design guess.
  5. At t_end (60 f or a wall) the ball disappears, play end_blast at the ball ground point (30 f), fire_patch at blast+2 (75 f life). Damage in a 46 px / 2.9 u radius (patch) while it burns, in a 22 px radius for the blast itself.
  6. Limit simultaneous bands: v2 spam turned bands into a wall; v3 keeps tile life 2 s and spawns at most 1 tile / 6 px. If many boulders are alive, scale tile alpha not size.

=====================================================================================================
6. WHAT THE IN-GAME v2 CODE / ART DID WRONG (HellordFireBasics1481.cs, HellordFireBasicsFX1481.cs, HellordTuning.cs)
=====================================================================================================
 - Scale: authored for a 17.5 px hero; the real hero is 23.3 px. Everything was ~25 % too small relative to the hero.
 - Boulder speed 75 px/s with a 12.6 px ball = 4.2 ball diameters/s (reference 2.5-3), and it covered the arena in 0.4-0.6 s. rrRange 70-150 px; rrCrumbleR = 1 m (16 px) far too small for a 2-3 H fire patch. Ball 12.6 px, low contrast.
 - Trail was one smooth ribbon (rounded top, no tongues, no sparks) instead of a band of 6 px tiles with tongues, each burning 1.4-3 s.
 - Spit glob lobbed along an arc instead of flying flat; 6-8 px orange sparkle, no dark rock; sort by y only so imps hid it.
 - Burst = pale-gold comb of straight hairlines (wider than +-40 deg) instead of ~8 dark-red wavy ropes with bright heads; no round fireball, no star flashes, no heat patch, no ember patches, scorch = purple scar lines.
 - One shared glow setting with the gold tint (ff963c) for everything, glow also lit the dark ball; draw was 1x unrotated; no cast ring / wind-up pose / launch.
 - Spam: overlapping bands/blasts every ~0.5 s -> wall. v3 adds the 2.0 s tile life, the ember cap, and clear sort rules.

=====================================================================================================
8. HONEST GAPS / CAVEATS
=====================================================================================================
 - UNTESTED IN UNITY. Mock-up glow is simulated (gaussian x tint), not the real FrostGlow shader; tune _Add/tint in game.
 - Reference character heights are +-15-20 % by eye (PoE2 tutorial camera is closer than Karzel); the reference panel scale was chosen as 0.55 (PoE2) and 0.933 (D2R).
 - The boulder range/duration are the brief's (2 s, 106 px); the README implies 6-8 characters. The D2R ball in the reference is shown at 0.73 H.
 - No verified hit/knock-back reference for either skill.
 - Look gaps I see vs the reference: (1) the D2R band is brighter, wider and fiercer than ours (ours is a calmer tiled band: more white-yellow core and taller flame bodies might be wanted in game with additive glow); (2) the reference blast chunks fly farther and are larger/darker against a green dirt floor, ours are 4 px chunks on blue ice; (3) PoE2 strands are many tiny sparks along the rope; ours are cleaner ropes; (4) ours has no ground-light cone on the hero/imps; (5) the mock-up hero is a cut-out of the real capture (a little fringe), imps are real sprites.
 - Imp hit reactions in the mock-up are only a nudge, not the game's real behaviour.
