# Hellord — Skill Mechanic Ideas Menu v0.2 (DESIGN ONLY)
Expands v0.1 (Branch/Ascend, Resonance, Corruption). Pick and mix. Every idea is written for Hellord: auto-cast skills, level-up cards, supports, readable mid-fight.
Cost: S = icons/text only, M = new VFX or a small UI panel, L = new systems, art sets, or big UI.

## 1. Skill evolve / branch / ascend
**1.1 Branch at Lv3** — At Lv3 a card offers 2 forms (e.g. Fireball → Meteor / Hellshot) that change behaviour, not just numbers. *Why:* identity per run, ~20 forms. *Early:* first branch about 3–5 min in, so floor 1 stays simple. *Cost:* M. *Ref:* Last Epoch skill trees, 20 Minutes Till Dawn upgrade trees.
**1.2 Ascension (VS Evolution)** — Lv5 skill + a specific support/trait gives a named ultimate form (Meteor + Burn → Rain of Ruin). Recipes are hinted as "???" in a codex. *Why:* discovery chase. *Early:* Lv5 needed, so floor 2+. *Cost:* M per skill. *Ref:* Vampire Survivors evolutions.
**1.3 Overclocks** — Rare cards that rewrite one rule of a skill with a trade-off: "Fireball: 1 giant slow ball, ×4 dmg", or "Storm Ring: rings pulse inward". *Why:* sidegrades create weird builds. *Early:* rare tier only, from room 5 on. *Cost:* S–M. *Ref:* Deep Rock Galactic overclocks / DRG Survivor.
**1.4 Mastery by use** — Each skill gains Mastery XP from kills. At thresholds you pick a small perk (a mini 3-node choice). *Why:* rewards sticking with a skill, and builds diverge by playstyle. *Early:* first node after about 150 kills. *Cost:* S. *Ref:* Halls of Torment ability upgrades, Diablo 4 skill nodes.

## 2. Support links & resonances
**2.1 Element Resonance** — 3/5 pieces of an element (Fire/Frost/Storm/Blood/Bone/Soul) turn on a rule (Fire 3 = burning enemies explode). HUD pips. *Why:* power spikes, mixed-element pivots. *Early:* hard to reach 3 on floor 1. *Cost:* S–M. *Ref:* TFT/Hades-style set bonuses, Soulstone Survivors tags.
**2.2 Linked supports (sockets)** — Supports are slotted INTO a skill (2 slots, a 3rd at Lv4). Effects apply only to that skill, and the placement choice matters. *Why:* real PoE gem-link decisions. *Early:* 1 slot at start. *Cost:* M (socket UI on the level-up card). *Ref:* PoE2 skill gems + support gems.
**2.3 Trigger Supports (meta)** — Supports like "Cast on Kill", "Cast on Freeze", "Cast when Hit" make one skill fire another automatically, through an Energy meter. *Why:* chain reactions, the PoE "combo" feel, very unpredictable. *Early:* trigger cards appear only from floor 2. *Cost:* M. *Ref:* PoE2 meta gems (Cast on Freeze, Cast on Shock), Invocation.
**2.4 Duo Supports** — If you own 2 specific supports, a rare Duo card appears (Burn + Shock → "Plasma": burning enemies chain lightning). *Why:* Hades-style surprise. *Early:* needs 2 supports Lv2+. *Cost:* S–M. *Ref:* Hades Duo Boons.
**2.5 Support levels as breakpoints** — Supports gain a special effect at Lv3 and Lv5 (Pierce Lv5: pierced enemies take ×1.5). *Why:* makes leveling a support exciting instead of +10%. *Early:* Lv1–2 plain. *Cost:* S.

## 3. Skill merge / fusion
**3.1 Twin Fusion** — Two maxed skills merge into one hybrid that frees a slot (Fireball + Chain Lightning → Storm Comet). One per run, at a boss altar. *Why:* big late identity. *Early:* boss reward only. *Cost:* L (unique VFX per pair; start with 3 pairs). *Ref:* VS Unions, Magicraft.
**3.2 Echo Graft** — Sacrifice a skill to graft its on-hit effect onto another (Frost Cleave grafted onto Soul Spears = spears chill + cone). *Why:* cheap fusion without new art. *Early:* Shrine only. *Cost:* S–M.

## 4. Weapon / relic ↔ skill synergy
**4.1 Hell Relics** — Relics (no weapon swap) drop from Elites and chests, with passives tied to tags ("Projectile skills fire backwards too", "Orbitals leave fire trails"). *Why:* item-driven builds, the Risk of Rain stacking feel. *Early:* the first relic is simple, e.g. +move speed. *Cost:* M (relic icons). *Ref:* Risk of Rain 2 items, Brotato items, PoE2 uniques.
**4.2 Unique Skill Modifiers** — Rare legendary drops that change a specific skill ("Bone Orbit bones explode when they hit"). *Why:* build-around moments. *Early:* boss drops only. *Cost:* S each. *Ref:* Diablo 4 Legendary Aspects / Uniques.

## 5. Conditional / stance / form changes
**5.1 Hell Forms (stance)** — Toggle or auto-switch between 2 forms: Demon Form (melee skills ×, slower move) and Wraith Form (projectiles ×, faster dash). Auto-switch on dash, or by HP threshold. *Why:* builds lean on one form or alternate. *Early:* unlock after floor 1 boss. *Cost:* M–L (hero art palette). *Ref:* PoE2 stances, Druid shapeshift (D4 / PoE2).
**5.2 Conditional bonuses** — Cards like "While above 80% HP: +50% area", "While moving: projectiles", "While standing still 1s: Storm Ring doubles". *Why:* playstyle-defining, more skill expression. *Early:* simple common cards are fine. *Cost:* S. *Ref:* PoE "while" mods, Halls of Torment traits.
**5.3 Rage / Blood Price** — Some skills can cost HP for ×dmg, gated by Life Leech builds. *Why:* risky high-reward builds. *Early:* opt-in card. *Cost:* S. *Ref:* PoE2 Blood Magic, Blood Mage.

## 6. Sacrifice / corruption / risk
**6.1 Corruption altar** (v0.1 C) — Random corruption of a skill: big up / twist element / brick. *Cost:* M. *Ref:* PoE Vaal Orb.
**6.2 Devil's Bargain cards** — Rare cards: "+1 projectile to all skills, −20% max HP", "Double XP, enemies +25% HP". *Why:* players choose their own difficulty, and builds snowball. *Early:* from floor 2. *Cost:* S. *Ref:* Brotato risky items, Hades Pact of Punishment.
**6.3 Banish / Reroll / Skip economy** — Bones pay for rerolls, banishing a card forever, or locking a card. *Why:* steering toward a build target makes long-tail builds reachable. *Early:* fine. *Cost:* S. *Ref:* VS Reroll/Banish/Skip, Brotato shop lock.

## 7. Combo chains / sequences
**7.1 Combo Marks** — Skill A applies a mark, skill B detonates it (Curse Rune marks, Chain Lightning detonates for ×3). Shown as glyph icons. *Why:* rewards skill pairing. *Early:* basic marks only from room 4. *Cost:* M. *Ref:* PoE2 combos (Primed, Detonate), D4 vulnerable.
**7.2 Elemental Reactions** — Status pairs react: Chill + Burn = Steam (AoE blind), Shock + Bleed = Hemorrhage, Burn + Bone = Pyre. *Why:* emergent, unpredictable chains. *Early:* single statuses on floor 1. *Cost:* M. *Ref:* Genshin reactions, PoE2 ailments.
**7.3 Cast Cadence** — Every Nth cast of a skill is Empowered (bigger, glows). Supports can change N. *Why:* readable rhythm with power spikes. *Early:* safe. *Cost:* S. *Ref:* Brotato/VS crit cadence, PoE2 Impending Doom-style cadence.

## 8. Passive tree lite
**8.1 Constellation Board** — Between floors, spend Sins (a meta currency per floor) on a small 3-branch board (about 12 nodes): Wrath (dmg), Gluttony (sustain), Pride (crit). *Why:* gives direction without a spreadsheet. *Early:* starts empty. *Cost:* M (one panel). *Ref:* PoE2 passive tree (lite), Soulstone Survivors talents.
**8.2 Keystones** — 1 per run, very build-defining: "Glass Cannon: ×2 dmg, 1 HP shield", "Iron Will: no crit, area ×1.5", "Chaos Inoculation". *Why:* run identity from a single choice. *Early:* first keystone at the floor 1 boss. *Cost:* S. *Ref:* PoE Keystones.

## 9. Elemental infusion / conversion
**9.1 Infusion cards** — Convert a skill's element ("Fireball → Frost": counts as Frost, chills). This lets a set bonus be completed. *Why:* flexible resonance completion. *Early:* uncommon from room 5. *Cost:* S–M (palette swap VFX). *Ref:* PoE2 Infusion/conversion supports, elemental conversion.
**9.2 Infuse via orbs** — Elemental orbs drop from themed demons (Frozen floor drops frost orbs). Picking one up infuses the next N casts. *Why:* themes affect builds, which fits the floor shuffle. *Early:* fine. *Cost:* S. *Ref:* PoE2 Elemental Infusion (Infusion buffs).

## 10. Minion / totem / trap skills
**10.1 Bound Demons (minions)** — New skill family: summon Imps that fight. Supports apply (Burn makes minions ignite). *Why:* a new archetype. *Early:* skill card from floor 1, but weak. *Cost:* L (minion art = reuse enemy sprites tinted). *Ref:* PoE2 minions, Halls of Torment summons.
**10.2 Totems / Shrines** — A skill places a totem that casts the linked skill. *Why:* turtle builds. *Early:* fine. *Cost:* M. *Ref:* PoE totems.
**10.3 Traps / Mines** — Auto-drops traps behind you while moving. Supports apply. *Why:* kite builds. *Early:* fine. *Cost:* M. *Ref:* PoE traps, Bombs (Brotato).

## 11. Ultimate / Hellfire meter
**11.1 Hellfire Ultimate** — A meter fills from kills. When full, auto-cast an ultimate based on your dominant element (Fire = Inferno nova). The meter evolves with Resonance tier. *Why:* a climax moment that is build-shaped. *Early:* weak nova on floor 1. *Cost:* M. *Ref:* Soulstone Survivors ultimates, D4 ultimates.
**11.2 Soul Overcharge** — Spend soul pickups to overcharge one skill for 10 s (×2 casts). *Why:* active decision, optional. *Cost:* S.

## 12. Level-up card rarity / build-defining choices
**12.1 Card rarities** — Common (stats), Rare (supports Lv-ups, conditionals), Epic (branch / duo / trigger), Legendary (ascension / keystone / overclock), with luck stat scaling. *Why:* readable excitement and a variance spike. *Early:* Epic odds 0 on floor 1 rooms 1–3. *Cost:* S (card frames). *Ref:* Brotato tiers, VS luck, Halls of Torment rarity.
**12.2 Build-Target cards ("Wishlist")** — Pick 1 target at run start (e.g. Rain of Ruin), and its components show up more often. *Why:* new players get guided toward builds. *Cost:* S.
**12.3 Floor Boons (big pick)** — After each boss, pick 1 of 3 huge boons (3 cards, each a run-defining rule). *Why:* fits the floor ladder. *Cost:* S–M. *Ref:* Hades boon choices, Slay the Spire boss relics.

## 13. Other strong research ideas
**13.1 Glyph Board / Gem Grid** — Supports placed on a small 3×3 grid touching skills, with adjacency bonuses. *Why:* spatial puzzle. *Cost:* L (risky menu-heavy). *Ref:* Backpack Battles, Last Epoch idols.
**13.2 Spirit Reservation (PoE2 Spirit)** — Auras/minions/triggers reserve Spirit, so build budget becomes a real constraint. *Cost:* S–M.
**13.3 Codex / Discovery log** — Evolutions and duos are revealed when found, which drives replay. *Cost:* M. *Ref:* VS collection.

## Top 5 recommendations (ranked)
1. **Branch at Lv3 + Ascension (1.1 + 1.2)** — the clearest "skill becomes something new" fix, and it fits level-up cards.
2. **Card rarities + Banish/Reroll (12.1 + 6.3)** — cheap, gives excitement and steering. Also gates power to later floors.
3. **Element Resonance + Elemental Reactions (2.1 + 7.2)** — emergent combos, unpredictable, mostly reuses existing statuses (Burn/Chill/Shock/Bleed).
4. **Trigger Supports (2.3)** — the core of PoE-like crazy chains; floor 2+ only.
5. **Floor Boons + Keystones (12.3 + 8.2)** — a run-defining choice after each boss, matching the floor ladder; very cheap.
*Later / optional:* Relics (4.1), Twin Fusion (3.1), Hell Forms (5.1), Minions (10.1).
