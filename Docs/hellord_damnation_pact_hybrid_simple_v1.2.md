# Damnation Pact — Combos (simple v1.2)
Replaces the hybrid docs v1.0 and v1.1.

## The rule
Each run, pick ONE: a **Path** or a **Combo**. Never both. Never two Paths.

## Path
- Pick up to 6 nodes, all from that one Path.
- Max 1 Keystone.
- Crest at 4+ nodes.

## Combo
- 6 ready-made kits that mix two Paths.
- No picking. The kit gets stronger by itself as your sockets grow: 2 → 4 → 6.
- No Path nodes, no Keystone, no Crest.
- **Unlock:** earn both parent Crests once, then pay Souls + 1 Sigil.

## The 6 Combos
| Combo | Mix | Effect | Drawback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Burning Bones | Fire + Bone | Your orbiting bones set enemies on fire and regrow on burn kills. | Projectiles are slower. |
| Plasma Storm | Fire + Storm | Shocking a burning enemy makes its fire jump as lightning. | Weak vs enemies that aren't burning or shocked. |
| Blood Marrow | Bone + Blood | Bones make enemies bleed and heal you. | Bones regrow slower. |
| Grave Conduit | Bone + Storm | Lightning jumps through your skeletons and zones. | Your own lightning jumps 1 fewer time. |
| Blood Lightning | Storm + Blood | Lightning heals you but costs HP to cast. | Can't heal above 80% HP. |
| Crimson Pyre | Blood + Fire | Fire on a bleeding enemy blows up all its bleed at once. | Fire is weaker on enemies that aren't bleeding. |
